# Lyvune v0.1.0-alpha.4

**More songs get lyrics, karaoke is chosen when it really fits, and the lyrics manager asks less often.** This alpha fixes why some lyrics were not found or not upgraded, and why searching felt slow. It is still an alpha: back up your data before every update.

## What changed

- **Lyrics that exist are found.**
  - A featuring credit outside brackets (`Song feat. Guest`, `Song ft. Guest`) no longer makes the right recording look like another one, and is no longer sent to the providers as part of the title. `Song feat. Guest (Live)` is still the live version.
  - Artist names with `&`, `,` or `/` are searched as they are: `Simon & Garfunkel`, `Earth, Wind & Fire`, `AC/DC`. Before, LRCLIB was asked for `Simon` or `AC`. When a credit list such as `Main, Guest` finds nothing, LRCLIB is asked once more for the first name.
- **Verified karaoke is preferred, and accuracy comes first.** Word-timed lyrics still win over line-synced ones when they match the recording. A result whose length differs from the track by more than 5 seconds is no longer taken automatically, however good its score: it may be another recording.
- **New lyrics are not hidden for long.**
  - "Nothing found" is remembered for 3 days instead of a week.
  - Turning a provider on, allowing it to be saved or lowering the match threshold looks again on the next play, both for songs without lyrics and for upgrade checks. Songs checked by an earlier version are looked at again once.
- **Fewer requests to the providers.**
  - After lyrics are found, the next play does not repeat the search.
  - A result that may be neither saved nor shown is remembered instead of being searched for again on every play.
  - Identical searches made at the same time share one request.
- **The reason a karaoke version is not used is shown.** In the lyrics manager, word-timed results and the one that would be taken say what playing the track does with them: chosen, or why not (another result ranks higher, the match is uncertain, the length differs, the provider is off or may not be saved or shown, the lyrics are locked or were added by hand, or automatic retrieval is off).
- **The browser keeps what it already has.**
  - Search results, empty ones included, are kept for 10 minutes and shown again when the manager is reopened. **Search again** always asks.
  - "No lyrics" in the player is kept for 5 minutes only.
  - After a provider did not answer, the next play of the song tries again.
  - The manager no longer shows the results of the previous song when the song changes during a search.

Your lyrics files, locks, offsets, sidecars and the translation settings from alpha.2 are unchanged. Two fields were added to the lyrics API (`auto` on search results, `skipped` on the resolve answer).

## Updating, backup and going back

There are **no database changes** in this release.

```sh
docker compose exec lyvune lyvune backup create       # first, a backup
# in .env: LYVUNE_IMAGE=ghcr.io/tian-mc/lyvune:0.1.0-alpha.4
docker compose pull && docker compose up -d --no-build
docker compose ps                                      # wait for "healthy"
```

To go back, set `LYVUNE_IMAGE` to `ghcr.io/tian-mc/lyvune:0.1.0-alpha.3` and run the same two commands. The older version ignores what this one adds to its notes about each song, and asks the providers again for a few songs at most. Details: [Updating, and going back](../DOCKER.md#updating-and-going-back).

## What was checked

The Go and web tests, the linters and the Pipeline passed on the commit before the tag. The image is tested on `amd64` and on a native `arm64` runner before it is pushed, and again after, pulled by its digest. Not tested on a real ARM board.
